The full re-audit against the live code found 86 real gaps across 70 cards. 22 of the straightforward ones closed in the first fix wave; you then answered the 9 card-specific questions from the "needs your call" list, and all 9 are built and tested. What's left below is genuinely down to two things: a handful of cards still needing a real new subsystem (a continuous board-wide aura; a "becomes a face-up Prophecy" conversion), and the rest are just plain unbuilt — no more silent typos or swallowed clauses hiding in the list.

Deja VuDone
Return target Being that you control with cost (X) to your hand, then Summon it without paying its summoning cost Pay (2) additional Timeless Essence to target a Deity.
Your four rulings, all built: the (X) in Deja Vu's own printed cost is the target's own total casting cost, read back at cast time — the first card whose own cost genuinely widens based on which target gets picked. toGameCard was silently dropping X-cost pips entirely before this (a real, pre-existing gap this card exposed); fixed with a new xCostColor marker. Only Beings (and Deities, with their own +2 Timeless surcharge) the player can actually afford the combined cost for are ever offered as legal targets. Casting it pays nothing up front — the whole combined cost is paid once a target is chosen, then that Being is returned and immediately resummoned on the exact same tile for free, unconditional and un-stoppable per your ruling, retriggering When Summoned like any real summon.
Immen Gorta's own summoning costDone
As an additional cost to summon, Sacrifice (2) Beings.
The last piece of Immen Gorta — everything about its board behavior (the Shift, the loop, the damage) was already built. Never offered at all without 2 of your own Beings already on board to pay it with. The normal Formless summoning cost is paid first, then clicking a highlighted Being sacrifices it — one at a time, same board-native click as every other target choice in the game — until 2 are picked, then Immen Gorta is placed for real and its own When Summoned fires.
UI gap found and fixed along the wayDone
shift-destination, shift-return, shift-from-purgatory-destination, force-shift-target, copy-opponent-effect, give-different-typed-buff, udarik-shift-target, echoes-boundless-shift-instead
While wiring up Deja Vu's own click-to-target picker, found that every one of these — real, working, fully tested at the engine level since the Shift round — had no actual button or highlight in the app itself. They'd have silently stalled a real game the moment a human player hit one. All eight now click the same way every other board choice does.
