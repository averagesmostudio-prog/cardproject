Scripturas Alpha — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Scripturas Alpha is a small, independently run card game. Creating an account is optional; you can play without one. This page explains what we store if you do create one.

What we collect

  • If you create a username and password: your username, and a salted one-way hash of your password. We never store or see your password itself.
  • If you sign in with Google: Google's internal ID for your account and your first name (used as your display name). We do not receive or store your email address, photo, or any other Google data.
  • Saved decks: the names and card lists of decks you save while signed in, so they can sync between your devices.
  • Online matches: while you play, the game server passes moves between you and your opponent. It does not keep a record of the game afterward. If you are signed in, your display name is shown to your opponent.
  • Server logs: like any website, our web server records technical details of each request (such as IP address and time). We use IP addresses briefly to limit repeated login attempts.

What we do with it

We use this information only to run the game: signing you in, showing your name in matches, and syncing your decks. We do not sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it for tracking or analytics.

Where it lives

Account data is stored on our own server. Decks you have not saved to an account stay in your own browser or app on your device.

Deleting your data

To have your account and synced decks deleted, email averagesmostudio@gmail.com with your username (or, for Google sign-in, the first name on the account), and we will remove it. Signing out removes the sign-in from that device immediately.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it.
